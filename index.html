Ryze LP Performance Checker

Paste any Base wallet address for an instant realized-APR report on its Ryze Protocol liquidity positions. Computed entirely in your browser from public data — nothing is uploaded or tracked.

Read-only: this page only queries public blockchain and price APIs. It never asks for a wallet connection or signature.

Methodology

  • Principal = cumulative (deposits − withdrawals) per pool, marked daily with Kraken closes (USDC = $1).
  • Deposits: transactions where the wallet sends USDC/WETH/cbBTC and the same transaction deposits LP into a Ryze pool. Plain swaps excluded; dust returned to the wallet is netted off.
  • Withdrawals: transactions that burn pool LP with underlying returned to the wallet.
  • Rewards = claimed (pool → wallet USDC transfers) + unclaimed (read live from each pool's claimable-rewards view).
  • Realized APR = rewards ÷ TWAP × 365/days. APY = (1+APR/52)52−1 (weekly compounding).
How time-weighted principal is computed, step by step
  1. Find the flows. Every deposit and withdrawal becomes a dated entry: date, pool, and token amounts (USDC / WETH / cbBTC). Claims never touch principal.
  2. Pool migrations net out. When a withdrawal is followed within 2 days by deposits into the same pool (e.g. a pool migration: withdraw from the old pool, deposit into the new one), the pair is netted to its capital delta — only genuinely new money counts as a deposit.
  3. Walk every day. Starting from the first activity day through today, each day's flows are applied to running per-pool balances, which are then marked to market: USDC counts at $1, WETH at that day's ETH close, cbBTC at that day's BTC close (Kraken daily closes, Coinbase Exchange fallback). Balances can't go below zero.
  4. Average it. Time-weighted principal (TWAP) is the average of those daily USD balances, per pool. The blended figure is the sum of both pools.
  5. Divide rewards by TWAP. Realized APR = total rewards ÷ TWAP × 365 ÷ days in the window.

Limits, stated plainly: principal is reconstructed from funds the wallet itself sends — deposits funded directly by a third party can't be attributed from transfer history alone. The figure is rewards-only: it measures reward payouts against principal, not total position P&L (no impermanent loss, no in-pool fee accrual).

Notes

  • Data: Blockscout (Base), Kraken (or Coinbase Exchange fallback) daily closes, Base public RPC — all client-side.
  • Analysis typically takes 20–60 seconds depending on wallet history.
  • Historical performance does not predict future returns. Not financial advice.
